*Last updated: 25 November 2026.*

This policy explains what data Backend Bootcamp (backendbootcamp.online) collects, why, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language. The short version: the site stores your lesson progress in your own browser, and with your permission uses Google Analytics to count visits. The site has no accounts, no ads and no tracking pixels.

Cristian Curteanu runs Backend Bootcamp and is the controller of the data described here. To ask a question or make a request, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/CristianCurteanu/backend-bootcamp/issues) or send a message on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cristian-curteanu/).

## Browser storage on your device

The site uses your browser's storage to remember how you use it. This data is created and kept on your device. It is not sent to our servers, and we cannot read it.

| Key | Storage | What it holds | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bb:consent:v1` | Local storage | Your cookie choice and the date you made it | Until you clear site data or change your choice |
| `bb_consent` | First-party cookie | A backup copy of your cookie choice, so the banner is not shown again if local storage is unavailable | 1 year |
| `bb:theme` | Local storage | Light or dark theme, if you chose one | Until you clear site data |
| `bb:progress:v1` | Session storage | Lessons you marked complete, checklist ticks, quiz answers and the last lesson you opened | Until you close the browser tab |

Because progress lives in session storage, it is deleted when you close the tab. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything else. You can reset progress for a tutorial at any time with the "Reset progress" button on its outline page.

## Google Analytics

With your consent, Backend Bootcamp uses Google Analytics 4, a service of Google LLC and, for visitors in the European Economic Area, Google Ireland Limited. It tells us how many people visit, which pages they read and how they found the site, so we can improve the tutorials.

**Nothing from Google Analytics loads or runs until you click "Accept all" or enable Analytics under "Customize" in the consent banner.** If you choose "Necessary only", no analytics script is requested and no analytics cookies are set.

When you accept, Google Analytics:

- sets the cookies `_ga` and `_ga_<container ID>`, which distinguish visitors and sessions. Both expire after up to two years;
- receives your page views, the pages you visit, approximate location (city or country level), device and browser type, screen size, language, referring site and the time you spend on pages;
- receives your IP address. IP anonymization is enabled, and Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses.

We do not send your name, email address or lesson progress to Google Analytics. Analytics data is processed by Google, including on servers outside your country, under Google's terms and its safeguards for international transfers. Event-level data is kept for the retention period configured in our Google Analytics property, which is never longer than 14 months. See [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Legal basis:** your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing is as easy as giving it.

**Opt out another way:** you can also install Google's [Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console to see how Backend Bootcamp appears in Google Search: which queries show the site, how often people click, and whether any pages have indexing problems. Search Console does not run on this website and sets no cookies in your browser. It reports data that Google already holds about searches on Google, in aggregated form, to the site owner. We cannot identify individual visitors from it. Site ownership is verified through a DNS record or an HTML meta tag, which carries no personal data.

## Google Fonts

The site's typefaces (Bricolage Grotesque, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono) are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. Google states that Google Fonts does not set cookies. This request is needed to display the site as designed, and happens before you make a consent choice.

## Newsletter

The subscribe form sends you to Substack, which handles your email address under [Substack's privacy policy](https://substack.com/privacy). We only receive your subscription through Substack. You can unsubscribe from any email.

## Server logs and hosting

Like any website, the host that serves Backend Bootcamp processes technical request data (IP address, requested page, browser and time) to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not use this data to identify you.

## External links

Pages link to other sites such as GitHub, LinkedIn and X. Those sites have their own privacy practices that we do not control.

## Your choices and rights

- **Change your cookie choice any time:** use "Cookie settings" in the footer of every page.
- **Delete browser storage:** clear this site's data in your browser settings.
- **Your rights.** Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because analytics data is held in Google Analytics and cannot be linked back to you by us, we can usually only act on a request by deleting data tied to your `_ga` client identifier if you share it with us.
- **Children.** Backend Bootcamp is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes in a way that affects your data, we update the date at the top and ask for your consent again if needed. Earlier versions are available in the site's [Git history](https://github.com/CristianCurteanu/backend-bootcamp/commits/main/content/privacy.md).
